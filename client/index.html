DISTORTION GRID GUIDE

How to Play

Rotate tiles 90° clockwise to discover the hidden route from the Distortion Core through every Crystal and into the Stabilizer. You do not need to use every tile — many are decoys. When the route reaches the Goal, the entire grid stabilizes. Fewer moves wins; fastest time breaks ties.

✦
Distortion CoreThe energy source. Every puzzle begins here.
◆
CrystalRequired checkpoint. Every crystal must receive energy.
◈
StabilizerThe final destination. Power it to finish.
━
ConduitRotatable energy path. Powered connections glow.

Difficulty Levels

Stable — 4×4 grid, 1 crystal, fewer branches.
Unstable — 5×5 grid, 1 crystal, more branches.
Fractured — 6×6 grid, 2 crystals, heavier branching.
Cataclysm — 7×7 grid, 3 crystals, most complex layout.

Distortion Ratings

Par is the target number of moves. A hidden Perfect Route uses even fewer moves, so exceptional solves can finish under Par.

✨ Perfect Stabilization — Exact minimum route.
🌌 Reality Break — 3 or more moves under Par.
🌀 Rift Mastery — 2 moves under Par.
⚡ Distortion Surge — 1 move under Par.
◆ Stabilized — Exactly Par.
⚠ Minor Instability — 1 move over Par.
⚠ Major Instability — 2 moves over Par.
🚨 Critical Instability — 3 moves over Par.
🚨 Unstable +4+ — 4 or more moves over Par.
